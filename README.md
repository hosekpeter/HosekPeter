## 🔎 How I approach production engineering

- **Reliability:** idempotency, retries, timeouts, and predictable failure handling.
- **Concurrency:** goroutines, channels, synchronization, and cancellation with context.
- **Observability:** **Datadog** for APM and metrics; **Sentry** for error tracking and debugging.
- **Maintainability:** clear APIs, automated testing, and simple designs that are easy to operate.
